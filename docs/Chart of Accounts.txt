Accounting Systems:

Accounting begins with a chart of accounts.  There are 5 types of accounts:  Income, expenses, assets, liabilities and equity.   
Income and expenses are used to record income and expenditure over time.  Income minus expenses shows a profit or loss and provides a statement of performance (usually known as a profit and loss).
Assets and liabilities are used to record what the entity owns and owes.  Assets minus liabilities provides a statement of position (usually known as a balance sheet)  The balance of assets minus liabilities is the equity.
