# Plan: Rename "Cash" → "Accrual" on P&L Pages

## Background
The system has moved to accrual accounting — the P&L reads from posted GL journal entries (FinanceJournalLine). The "Cash" view mode terminology is outdated and should be renamed to "Accrual" to reflect the actual accounting method.
